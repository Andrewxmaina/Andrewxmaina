## 🚀 About Me

> *"Turning code into stories, and stories into code."*

I'm a **persistent, methodical developer** from Kenya 🌍 who loves digging into both the *why* and the *how* of technical problems.
My work follows a **user-centric design philosophy**, from refining PHP CLI logic to exploring AI agent design and cryptography.

- 🎓 Final-year **Business Information Technology** student
- 💻 Building web apps, Android apps and PHP tools, with a tester's eye for breaking things before users do 🐞
- 🎨 Digital artist and aspiring animator who writes stories on the side
- 🏃 Run 5 km, three times a week (code stays clean, lungs stay clean)
- 🔵 Chelsea fan through and through
